## New

- Move comp and layer markers in the work area, the whole comp, or before or after the playhead, by frames or seconds, with Shift for ten times as far and Alt for the other way.
- Split markers: move each end of a duration marker separately.
- Move markers to the playhead, snap them to frames, and delete them in bulk.
- Copy, cut and paste markers between the comp and any number of layers, at their own times or the playhead.
- Five memory banks of markers, kept between sessions and projects.
- Save a work area and apply it again, here or on another comp.
- A guide layer copying the comp markers, so J and K jump between them.
- Marker trigger expressions, with delay, duration, reverse, fit to marker duration and a name filter.
- Import markers from a CSV file, including Reaper's region/marker export.
- Pull markers and regions straight from Reaper's web interface, and update them by importing again.
- Export markers as a CSV file.
